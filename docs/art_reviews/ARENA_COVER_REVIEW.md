# Arena cover — the field's own cover art (2026-09-22)

The arena round gave a run four fields and two kinds of outcrop: a **standing** outcrop that stops bodies *and*
arrows, and a **low** one that stops only bodies. Both were drawn with the crystal landmarks, which is exactly the
wrong answer — a crystal says "this is the edge of the arena", and cover has to say "walk behind me, and your
arrows will stop here too" (or "…and your arrows will fly over me").

This batch is the fix: four families, three variants each, built from the arena's own stone sheet.

| Family | Kind | Construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `standing_stone` | shelter | tapered monolith, chiselled bands, capstone, flanking rock, moss footing |
| `ruin_slab` | shelter | leaning slab, broken course, rubble bed, ring memory |
| `thorn_hedge` | low | arched branches, thorn rake, moss clumps, low mound |
| `mossy_boulder` | low | rounded core, shoulder facet, lichen plates, scattered pebbles |

## Why the cover has its own batch

The arena batch draws the backdrop, the ground and the landmarks. Re-rendering those to move the cover would have
been a second, unrequested art change — and an uneven one, because the second arena's duplicates (`*_3`, `*_4`,
`*_5`, and `arena_backdrop_2`) are not part of that batch, so one half of a run would have moved tiers and the
other would not. The cover therefore renders alone (batch `arena-cover`), is reviewed alone, and is promoted alone;
the arena keeps the pixels it was reviewed with.

## What was measured

The audit (`arena_cover/arena_cover_audit.json`) is the evidence, and these two sheets are its pictures:

| Sheet | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `arena_cover_families.png` | the four families, three variants each, with a silhouette at field scale and a stage grade |
| `arena_cover_kinds.png` | standing cover against low cover at field scale, with the measured height separation |

Gates that fail the audit rather than being read off it: the candidate is exactly the twelve keys; every entry
states `arena-obstacle-premium-v1`, its family, its kind of cover and `runtimeGlow: false` (ground that glowed would
claim to be a landmark); every sheet is 384 px square, paints something and keeps a transparent margin; no two
sheets are the same image; the batch stays inside its decoded-memory budget; and the **shortest standing family has
to paint at least 1.6× the tallest low family**, because that separation is the rule the player is reading from
across the field.

## Decision

**Decision:** ACCEPTED — the twelve props are the field's cover, and the two kinds are separated by silhouette
rather than by hue.

| What | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Batch | `arena-cover-v1`, rendered by `generate-visual-assets.yml` run `35737207100` |
| Assets | 12 (4 families × 3 variants; 6 shelter, 6 low) |
| Revision label | `arena-obstacle-premium-v1` |
| Sheets | 384 px square, minimum transparent margin 40 px, `runtimeGlow: false` on every entry |
| Shortest standing cover vs tallest low cover | 193 px against 108 px = **1.787×** (floor 1.6×) |
| Decoded bytes | 7,077,888 of a 7,077,888-byte budget (the batch is exactly its own twelve sheets) |
| Audit sha256 | `d6ad4d3cf5840587e3abbfcaf9265034896777b1f84b3693f72816d13b9f3bff` |
| Candidate manifest sha256 | `fc4daa038d78dc50a69884e424232d942a1a1f5c9706ef8219bb07a5d99ea1ab` |

The audit and both sheets live in `docs/art_reviews/arena_cover/`, and `promote_arena_cover_batch.py` refuses to
copy a pixel whose sha256 is not in that audit.
