## 1. Method, and what this audit did not do

Done: read the tree at `bf3776e`; re-ran the measurement tool; ran `:core:test` and `:core:ciStaticAnalysis`
(193 classes / 816 tests / 0 failures / 27.9 s, and the analyzers green); checked the shipped asset inventory
against the manifest and the ledger; read the localisation layer's tests and its golden vectors; checked the
two CI jobs' state at the pushed commits.

Not done, and therefore not scored: playing the game by hand; a physical Android device (the emulator in CI is
the closest thing this repository has, and it is a device for input and lifecycle, not for how a wave feels);
any judgement of whether the Persian reads *well* to a Persian reader — this audit can prove the pipeline
reproduces 245 shaping vectors codepoint for codepoint and that 181 strings have both languages, and it cannot
prove the translations are idiomatic. That is Gate 2's territory and it needs a human.

One limitation stated up front rather than buried: the party that wrote the code since `12e4dc0` also wrote
this re-audit. Every score below is therefore traceable to a measurement or a named artefact, and where a
sub-item could only be judged by reading code, this document says so instead of converting the reading into a
number.

**Persian and RTL, 15 of 25, itemised because it moved the most and is the least finished.**

Earned: a shaping and bidi pipeline (`render/PersianShaper`, `render/BidiReordering`) that reproduces all 245
golden vectors codepoint for codepoint, where both the vectors and the three lookup tables are re-derived in CI
from `arabic_reshaper` 3.0.1 and `python-bidi` 0.6.11 at pinned versions before the JVM starts, so neither can
drift into being stale evidence; Vazirmatn Bold and ExtraBold committed under the SIL OFL with the glyph set
derived from what the tables actually put on screen; 10 string tables holding 181 entries, each entry both
languages in one constructor, so a string cannot be added with only one of them; `GameNumbers` for digits,
thousands separators and percentages, which is why a Persian sentence never carries a Latin digit; a language
row in settings, persisted, with each language named in its own script; mirrored layout on five screens
(settings, menu, pause overlay, run summary, and now the Root Network's close button with its tap target); and
`DrawnStringProvenanceTest`, a gate that fails the build when a source draws its own words, whose
`NOT_YET_MIGRATED` list is a ratchet checked in both directions.

Not earned, and this is the honest part: the list still names **17 files**, roughly 250 strings, including
every remaining overlay (Inventory 49, Stat Shop 29, Level Up 20, Codex 17, Reward Card 13) and every content
enum (Skill Evolution 28, Trophy 24, Reward Card 15, Epilogue 15, Affix 12, Skill 10). The **HUD does not
mirror** — it is the most-seen screen in the game, and it is the one screen whose layout is not a set of insets
inside symmetric containers: its two info panels span 18–414 of 720, its bars are inset 73/31 inside their
panel, its five buttons are asymmetric, and mirroring it means also reversing the direction its two progress
bars fill. That has to be one commit with the drawing, `HudTouchLayout` and the bar direction together, and it
has not been attempted rather than attempted blind. And the gate has a **blind spot**: its content rule reads
enum constructor arguments, so `ascension/RootNetworkCatalog` (23 node names and their bonus lines) and
`items/EquipmentCatalog` (every item name, which reaches the anvil's feedback through `%1$s`) are not caught by
it and are not translated.

## 8. The limits of this audit

A rubric measured against artefacts is not a review of the game as a player meets it. It cannot see whether the
coaching is understood, whether the Persian reads naturally, whether the mirrored screens look right on a
phone, or whether the boss music lands. Where this document credits a sub-item on the strength of a test
count, the test count is what was measured — 21 onboarding tests do not prove the onboarding teaches. The three
scores in this repository (505, 731, 777) come from the same ten categories at the same weights, so the
differences are work rather than a new ruler; the +46 here is 29 points of category 7, 8 of category 8, 6 of
category 2, 2 of category 5 and 1 of category 10, and every one of those is traceable to a row above.
